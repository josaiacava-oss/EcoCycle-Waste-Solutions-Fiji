# EcoCycle - Waste Solutions Fiji

## 🌍 Project Overview

EcoCycle is a comprehensive waste collection and recycling service business operating in Fiji. This project manages waste collection operations, customer relationships, recycling processes, and environmental impact tracking.

## 📋 Business Mission

To provide efficient, reliable, and eco-friendly waste collection and recycling services to residential and commercial clients throughout Fiji, promoting environmental sustainability and reducing landfill waste.

## 🏭 Key Features

- **Waste Collection Management** - Schedule and track collection routes
- **Customer Portal** - Easy booking and account management
- **Recycling Tracking** - Monitor recycled materials and impact
- **Route Optimization** - Efficient collection route planning
- **Analytics & Reporting** - Environmental impact metrics and business analytics
- **Billing System** - Automated invoicing and payment processing
- **Mobile App** - On-the-go collection management

## 🏢 Business Categories

- Residential waste collection
- Commercial waste management
- Recycling services
- Hazardous waste handling
- Bulk waste collection
